# ADR-XXXX: <short imperative title>

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-XXXX
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Serves:** REQ-xxx, REQ-yyy

## Context

What forces are at play? What constraint, cost, or failure mode makes this a real decision rather than an obvious default? State facts and numbers, not preferences.

## Decision

What we will do, stated actively: "We will …". One decision per ADR.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and the specific reason it lost. An ADR with no rejected alternatives usually wasn't a decision.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what new work this creates. Include the bad consequences — those are the ones worth knowing later.

## Revisit trigger

The concrete, observable condition under which we reopen this. Must be measurable.
