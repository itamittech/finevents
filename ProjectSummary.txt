WHY NO AGENTS (point 8)
  Examined and declined in ADR-0041. Scraper agents would duplicate Firecrawl,
  whose extraction is already LLM-driven. The wiki curator was the real candidate,
  but three of its four proposed tools collapsed into better prompt assembly, and
  the fourth -- query history to test a hunch -- does not fix its target risk: a
  model that never thinks of a hypothesis will not query for it either. Replaced
  by 1-hop link pre-loading and a nightly deterministic sweep of the entire
  event x instrument x horizon grid. Cheaper, and more complete.
  Consequence: this is a PIPELINE, not an agentic system. AgentDesign.md in the
  document list above no longer has a subject -- SystemDesign.md 2.1 covers the
  agency boundary instead.

THE BIGGEST DECISION NOT IN THE ORIGINAL BRIEF
  Forward-only (ADR-0037). The agent never runs against a historical date -- no
  backtest, no replay, at any scope. History feeds only numeric calibration, the
  wiki seed, and threshold tuning. This retired 7 of 11 leakage vectors, made
  time-series pre-training contamination structurally impossible rather than
  merely bounded, and cut one-off setup from ~$38 to ~$4. The price is patience.

HOW IT IMPROVES DAY BY DAY (points 6, 11, 12)
  Two arms, both arithmetic, both read back the next day:
    - The wiki learns about the WORLD -- evidence accretes, posteriors tighten,
      hypotheses get rewritten when they stop holding.
    - Calibration learns about the PREDICTOR -- reliability, directional bias and
      departure discipline fed back into the prompt, plus a mechanical calibration
      layer scored as its own ladder rung (0042). The wiki cannot see systematic
      predictor error; this is what catches it.

BUILD PLAN -- Tasks.md, 13 phases. Three orderings are non-negotiable:
  1. Bitemporal schema first. knowledge_time cannot be retrofitted onto
     populated stores.
  2. The leakage harness (Phase 4) gates live running. Under forward-only a
     leaked prediction cannot be re-run -- there is no second attempt at a day.
  3. The classification spot-check gates the wiki seed. A misclassification
     propagates into eleven years of seeded evidence.

FINAL THREE DECISIONS (ADR-0043, 0044, 0045)
  Steering (point 14) -- four verbs: flag, propose, edit, correct. Cognito-gated,
    fully audited. Human edits are allowed AND provenance-tracked, so skill is
    reported twice: on agent-authored pages and on human-touched pages. Full
    power and an honest claim, instead of trading one for the other.
  Licence -- Apache 2.0 for code. Derived data IS published: the full prediction
    and scoring record, wiki pages, severity scores, manifests, steering audit.
    Raw scraped article text is NEVER redistributed. Publishing the record is
    what makes a null result verifiable by someone else, and it makes steering
    self-policing -- you cannot quietly steer if the audit is public.
  Tuning window -- 60 trading days, ending when the configuration freezes (the
    shadow A/B concludes at day 60). Results are published but labelled `tuning`
    and excluded from headline skill. The wiki learns throughout. No extension.

  Consequence: first agent skill number arrives ~month 11-13, not 8-10.
